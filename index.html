About us

InternPath is an online internship portal for students. You choose a course, complete real tasks assigned by our team, and submit your work for review.

When all your tasks are approved and your fee is paid, you receive an internship certificate with a unique ID that anyone can verify on this website.

Questions? Email us at internpath.com@gmail.com.

Terms & Conditions

  1. Certificate eligibility: You receive the InternPath internship certificate only when you have completed all the tasks assigned to you and they have been approved, and you have paid the applicable fee.
  2. Both conditions are required: Completing the tasks without paying the fee, or paying the fee without completing the tasks, does not qualify you for a certificate.
  3. Fee: The fee amount and payment method will be shared with you by InternPath at the time of enrolment. Please pay only through the official channels shared by InternPath.
  4. Task review: All submitted work is reviewed by InternPath. Tasks may be returned for changes, and the certificate is issued only after final approval.
  5. Original work: The work you submit must be your own. Copied or plagiarised work may be rejected, and the certificate may be withheld or cancelled.
  6. Verification: Every certificate carries a unique ID that anyone can check on this website.
  7. Contact: For questions about courses, payments or certificates, write to our official email: internpath.com@gmail.com.

Privacy

When you register, we collect your name, email address and chosen course. We also store the links to the work you submit and your task progress.

We use this information only to run your internship, review your tasks and issue your certificate. A certificate's ID, your name and the issue date can be seen by anyone who verifies that ID.

To ask about your data, email internpath.com@gmail.com.
